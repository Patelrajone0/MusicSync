How do I connect multiple phones or speakers together? Popular

One person creates a room to get a 6-digit code. Friends tap Join Room and enter the code. As soon as music plays, all devices stream in exact millisecond sync.

Why is there an "Unlock Audio" prompt on iPhone or Chrome? Crucial

Mobile browsers require a physical screen tap before web audio can play. Tap anywhere on screen or hit "Unlock Audio" once to activate sound.

What if my Bluetooth speaker has a delay? Bluetooth

Bluetooth hardware adds 100-200ms latency. Inside any room, open Settings and adjust your personal "Device Offset Slider" to align phone and Bluetooth speakers perfectly.

Local Wi-Fi Mode vs Public Cloud Mode Network

Local Wi-Fi offers near-zero latency for devices on the same router or hotspot. Public Cloud relays audio across the internet for long-distance listening.
