Help & Quick Start Guide

Everything you need to synchronize audio across devices

🔊 Browser Audio Autoplay Unlock

Mobile browsers (iOS Safari & Chrome) require a physical screen tap before audio is unlocked. Tap anywhere on screen if you see the "Unlock Audio" prompt.

🚀 3 Steps to Sync

1. Host Creates Room: Tap "Create Room" to generate a 6-digit code or QR code.
2. Guests Join: Friends open MusicSync on their phones and enter the 6-digit code.
3. Hit Play: Add tracks to the queue. When the host plays, all devices play in millisecond sync!
